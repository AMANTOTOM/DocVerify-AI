# Motivation
## Time
Going through thousands of applications by hand takes a huge amount of time. For example, a large university might receive 5,000 applications, and if it takes roughly 15 minutes to check a folder of 8 to 10 documents, that adds up to about 1,250 hours of work.

## Errors
Handling, for example, 40 applications a day makes staff really tired. When someone is tired, they might miss an expired date or make a mistake converting a foreign grade. Separately, different reviewers might judge the same rule in different ways, which leads to inconsistent choices.
## Risks
If a fake document slips through, it damages the university's reputation and takes a spot away from a good student. If a qualified applicant gets rejected by mistake, it causes stressful delays right before the deadline. Doing all of this by hand also creates concrete privacy risks, since documents containing names, passport details, and grades get copied, emailed, and stored in many places.
## Why AI
Application documents come in all kinds of layouts, formats, and languages, so rigid computer rules usually break. AI is a good fit because it can read messy text and pull out the right info. Specifically, DocVerify AI checks for what matters in German applications: recognised institutions, grade conversion to the German scale, language levels, and the APS certificate. DocVerify AI is designed to use RAG to check official rules while showing its sources, and it has strict safety guards, like marking things as "Needs Review" instead of auto-rejecting, so a human always makes the final call. At the same time, the system is kept honest and does not claim to detect forged stamps or images.
