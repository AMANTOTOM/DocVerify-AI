# Scenarios

## Aysha's scenario
It is 10:30 PM on a Tuesday, and Aysha is sitting at her desk at home after a long day of work. With no university offices open, she logs into DocVerify AI to check her application documents before the approaching deadline. She uploads her degree certificate, language scores, and mandatory APS certificate, watching as the system checks her institution against its local anabin-based knowledge base. A dashboard appears showing her extracted data mapped to the German grading scale alongside confirmation that her APS certificate is present with an intact digital seal. However, the system flags one page of her semester mark sheet as "Needs Review" because the scan is too blurry to read clearly. She replaces the blurry page with a clean PDF export and resubmits, closing her laptop knowing her paperwork is in order.

## Albin's scenario

Albin sits on his bed with his laptop open, nervously staring at the university application portal three days before the deadline. Hoping to avoid mistakes, he logs into DocVerify AI and uploads a folder containing his transcripts, language certificate, and bachelor degree. When he checks the status, he sees that one of his transcripts was uploaded as a blurry phone photo with cut off edges, marked as "Needs Review." He pauses, re scans the document properly using a scanner app, and uploads the clear version. The system updates the status to show a high confidence score and verified fields, meaning his documents are now ready for the university's final admission evaluation. He saves his confirmation page and closes his laptop for the night.

## Markus's scenario

It is peak admission season, and Markus is staring at a queue of over 40 dense international applications waiting for review. Drained from hours of manual checks, he opens the DocVerify AI reviewer dashboard to manage his workload. The system displays the applications, marking the straightforward files as "Verified" with high confidence scores based on matching knowledge-base rules, while isolating the edge cases under a "Needs Review" tab. Markus quickly scans through the verified files to confirm them, then clicks into a flagged application where a foreign grading scale was unconventional. The system points him directly to the relevant rule snippet and source citation, helping him verify the conversion quickly. He approves the file and moves on to the next one, maintaining a steady and manageable pace through his queue.

## Sofia's scenario

Ahead of the new admission cycle, Sofia logs into the DocVerify AI admin dashboard to update the local institutional knowledge base with the latest anabin entries. She attempts to upload a newly formatted JSON rule package, but the system rejects the file due to a syntax error in one of the data fields. Sofia opens the error log, identifies the missing bracket in the file, corrects the syntax on her machine, and successfully re uploads the package. She then verifies that user role permissions are correctly assigned before logging out knowing the system is fully configured.
